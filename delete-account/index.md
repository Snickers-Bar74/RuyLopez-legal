# Delete your Ruy Lopez account and data

You can delete your account directly in Ruy Lopez:

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Choose **Delete my account and data**.
3. Read and confirm the deletion prompt. Use an internet connection so the
   app can delete server data as well as local data.

This deletes your leaderboard entry, nickname, rated results and the data on
this phone. The app removes your account identifier from name reports you made;
the reports stay until reviewed. Deletion cannot be undone. Uninstalling alone
removes local app data and does not request deletion of an online account.

If you cannot use the app, request deletion by emailing
[jonathan@nullmening.org](mailto:jonathan@nullmening.org?subject=Ruy%20Lopez%20account%20deletion)
with the subject **Ruy Lopez account deletion**. Include your leaderboard
nickname, if you chose one, and explain which record you want deleted. Do not
send passwords or access tokens. We must verify ownership before deleting a
record and may need to ask for more information. A public nickname alone does
not prove ownership.

Emailing cannot erase local data from a phone we do not control. Use the app's
deletion command or uninstall it to remove that local copy.
